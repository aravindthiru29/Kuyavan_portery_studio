Our Process

From Clay to Craft

Each Kuyavan pot begins as raw earth and becomes a piece of heritage — shaped, sun-dried, kiln-fired and hand-painted by our artisans in Tamil Nadu.

1

Raw Earth Selection

We source fine terracotta clay from the banks of the Kaveri river.

2

Hand Throwing

Each pot is thrown on the wheel by skilled kuyavars with decades of experience.

3

Sun Drying & Kiln Firing

Pieces are slow-dried, then fired at 900°C in traditional wood-fired kilns.

4

Hand Painting & Finishing

Natural pigments and traditional motifs are applied by hand — no two pieces alike.
